# Transparency and reproducibility statement

1. **Protocol:** The analysis plan was prespecified in sequential feasibility, linkage, covariate, and modeling phases; a concise protocol summary is included in this release. It was not prospectively registered.
2. **Preregistration:** No prospective registration was performed.
3. **Data access:** MIMIC-IV and MIMIC-IV-ECG require PhysioNet credentialing, approved training, and acceptance of the applicable data use agreement. Patient-level and stay-level derived files are not redistributed.
4. **Code sharing:** The release contains the HC3, precision-weighted, and empirical-calibration verification code, aggregate results, and figure-generation code. Upstream extraction must be performed locally by an authorized user.
5. **Reporting guidance:** The manuscript was reviewed against STROBE and RECORD. A section-level crosswalk is included.

No public DOI or GitHub release URL is asserted in this package. Add those identifiers after the corresponding records are created.
